# The map is one open office floor with a bench per project; v1 art is placeholder

The look follows open-plan call-centre floors (the film "No More Bet") so it reads nothing like Party Agent's cozy animal office, while reusing Party Agent's proven single-floor engine (placement, pathfinding, walking). Each Project owns one long 4-seat Bench, Benches stack in two columns, and workers sit with their backs to the camera so their screens carry the status; we rejected one-room-per-project because it fragments the floor and does not scale past ~8 projects at a readable zoom. v1 draws Pods and workers as code-generated pixel placeholders and reads every sprite through `public/office/manifest.json`, so Codex's art (brief in `docs/art-brief.md`) drops in without code changes. Party Agent's art and styling are not reused.
