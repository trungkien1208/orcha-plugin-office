1 · The floor planParty Agent's engine, new layout
  • One Project = one numbered team on a long bench of 4 seats, everyone facing the front wall. The floor tag reads TEAM 02 · acme-superapp.
  • Benches fill two columns in Orcha's project order. A new Project adds a new bench at the end; the floor grows downward.
  • Every Project keeps its bench, even with no agents: empty chairs, dark screens.
  • A new Agent walks in from the door, down the aisle, and sits at its bench's first free seat. An ended Agent stands up and leaves.
  • More than 4 Agents: a second bench appears in the next free place at the end, tagged name_1 / name_2. Benches never grow past 4 seats and never move while the page is open.
  • Leaving: two HR bouncers carry an ended Agent out of the door. Closing a Project: its bench burns (cartoon fire) to a scorch mark until reload.
  • Screens carry the status: blue code = working, gold flash = needs you, green ✓ = done, dark = idle, grey static = unknown. From behind, the screen is what you read first.
  • Uniform = Project colour, so a crowded floor still reads by team.
  • Front wall: red slogan banner, KPI leaderboard ranking teams by finished work (red flower for #1), a gong struck on every Done, clock, CCTV. Outside the windows: compound wall, palm trees, casino neon.
  • The bait: an "EASY JOB · HIGH PAY" recruitment poster by the door, the lure from Thiên đường máu and the real scam-compound stories.
  • Compound props: phone racks at every seat, stacked instant-noodle boxes and energy-drink crates, a dorm door with bunk beds, cash stacks and a money counter in the boss's office.
  • The boss (you): black shirt, sunglasses, walkie-talkie, patrols the aisle.
  • The map is always on the left of Home; clicking a worker selects it in the manager panel on the right.

3 · Mood from the filmcolour notes, not copied frames
Cold fluorescentCeiling light with a green-teal cast over grey floors and pale walls. Bright, flat, no warm light.
Office hardwarePale grey desks with dark edges, black mesh chairs, rows of identical dark monitors.
Slogan redLong red banners with cream lettering; the only warm colour on the walls.
Neon outsideNight windows: tall compound wall, palm trees, casino neon. Seen through glass only.
  • Keep the room bright and readable; the darkness and neon stay outside the windows.
  • Recognisable by its props (team numbers, slogans, leaderboard, gong, phone racks, noodles, bunks, the boss), not by violence: no bars, guards, weapons or punishment.

Film facts: No More Bets (Wikipedia), China Daily HK, Thiên đường máu (SGGP). No reliable source documents the film's palette; these colours are our own reading of its stills.

48×72 frames, feet on y=71. On the floor workers are seen from behind (↑), so the seated set is all back views. Polo in greys, tinted per Project. 
Pixel art game asset, modern indie quality (1px dark navy #2f3a4f outlines, flat 2-tone shading, no anti-aliasing, transparent background). Top-down three-quarter RPG camera (not isometric), 32px tile grid.
Setting: a huge open-plan call-centre floor in the mood of the film "No More Bets": long benches of four seats side by side, everyone facing the front wall, a monitor and desk phone at every seat, thin privacy panels between seats, pale grey desks with dark edges, black mesh chairs, grey-teal floor tiles under cold green-tinted fluorescent light, pale walls with long red slogan banners (cream pseudo-text), a KPI whiteboard, a wall clock, ceiling CCTV domes, night windows with purple and pink neon outside. Bright and readable inside. No bars, no guards, no weapons.
Workers are humans (not animals), chibi-ish proportions, matching polo shirts with a lanyard ID badge, mostly seen from behind while seated. Draw the polo in neutral greys only so it can be tinted at runtime.
